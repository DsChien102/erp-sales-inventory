## 7. Validation và phản hồi

### Result states

- `COMPLETED / ZERO_VARIANCE_NO_ADJUSTMENT`: final count khớp book; không adjustment; lock đã release.
- `COMPLETED / ADJUSTED`: authoritative adjustment ID + committed lines/time; session completed; lock release.
- `CANCELLED`: no adjustment; evidence retained; lock release.
- `REJECTED_EXECUTION`: effect NONE.
- `UNKNOWN`: finality chưa chứng minh; giữ same command/session identity, handoff/verify BJ17. Không retry adjustment/cancel bằng identity mới.
- `PERMISSION_DENIED`: restricted data absent khỏi response/DOM.
