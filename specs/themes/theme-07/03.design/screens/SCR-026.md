### Nội dung source còn lại / revision locks

### v1.4.1 — Inventory Operations UX correction

- Numeric picked progress là primary; normal progress badge không lặp lại cùng fact.
- LOT supporting copy rút về `Lô X` hoặc `N lô`; không dump per-allocation picked ratio ở overview.
- Source-waiting detail là read-only và không có item navigation affordance.

### v1.4.2 — Partial-handover affordance

- Progress vocabulary dùng `dòng đã lấy đủ` thay cho `mục`.
- Khi source cho partial handover, CTA `Rà soát bàn giao` được giải thích ngay trên screen bằng quiet decision note: **có thể bàn giao phần đã lấy; phần còn lại tiếp tục ở đơn này**.
- Không dùng banner cho normal partial allowance; banner vẫn dành cho stale/shortage/blocker.

### v2.7.0 — Figma DS expression

- Order/customer/warehouse facts use `DS v2 / Info Row`; Product+UOM tasks use independent `List Row`. Progress stays numeric; no routine progress badge/card duplication.
- Business state, authority, commit/finality and recovery rules above are unchanged.

### v2.7.3 — Collection consistency correction

- Product+UOM rows remain one `DS v2 / List Row` Navigation collection in every order state.
- In `SHORTAGE`/source-waiting or for an already completed line, the row uses `State=Disabled` while retaining `Type=Navigation`, `Density=Default`, `370×76` and the same trailing chevron anatomy as its peers.
- Disabled means the destination is temporarily unavailable from the current state; it must not be represented by switching the row to `Type=Status` or by removing only the chevron.

### v3.9.3 — Deep-review semantic closure

- Progress uses derived `picked_not_consumed / remaining_to_handover`; source requested quantity is immutable. Stale correction copy is data-driven from affected allocations, never hard-coded.
